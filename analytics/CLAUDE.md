# Analytics — Page Context

Metrics across playtime and achievements. Everything is computed in the browser, in the viewer's timezone (`assets/time.js`), from the files below.

## Data Sources (all fetched on mount, `loadAll()`)
- `../data/{ra,steam,xbox}/history/index.json` → the listed `{YYYY}.json` files: all-time unlocks `{ t, g, a, n, p?, r?, hc? }`
- `../data/{ra,steam}/playtime/{YYYY}.json` for every year from `PLAYTIME_FIRST_YEAR` (2026) to now: play sessions + `games` name/icon (RA also `console`)
- `../data/{ra,steam,xbox}/profile.json`: completions (RA `Mastery/Completion` awards, Steam/Xbox `perfectGames`), funnel counts, RA console names (`gameAwardsAndProgress`, `recentlyPlayedGames`, awards)
- `../data/{steam,xbox}/win-conditions.json`: beaten games for the funnel
- `../data/{steam,xbox}/games/index.json`: names, icons and current `unlocked`/`total` per game; Steam "started" count and farm detection (`playtimeForever`). RA current progress comes from `gameAwardsAndProgress` (`numAwarded` / `maxPossible`)
- Not `data/ra/games.json` (5 MB). No genre breakdown for that reason

The hub's Analytics strip (`loadAnalyticsStrip()` in `../index.html`) shows this page's 30-day hours, unlocks, top part of the day and most played game, computed the same way. Change the period rules, `DAY_PARTS`, the farm rule or the idle list handling here, and update it there too.

Game metadata is merged into `meta[platform][id]` (string ids) from all of the above.

## Period
One selector in the header scopes every section: 30 days · 3 months · 12 months · This year · All time, kept in the URL as `?period=` (absent = 30 days). Ranges are inclusive local day keys (`buildRange`); the previous period is the same length before it ("This year" compares with the same days of last year; All time has none). 3 months and 12 months are calendar months including the current one. Trend buckets: day (30 days), week keyed by its Monday (3 months; the first week can start before the range, only in-range days count), month (12 months, this year), year (all time; playtime falls back to months since tracking started).

**Platform** (All · RA · Steam · Xbox, next to the period; `?platform=`, absent = All) scopes every section through the same `keep()` as the farm filter. `units` (the platforms shown) and `plays` (those with playtime) drive the chart series, legends and tables (`seriesLegend`, `seriesTable`), the funnels, and which Overview tiles show (RA points with RA, gamerscore with Xbox). With one platform, Time of day drops the combined row; RA by console shows for All and RA only. Xbox has no playtime (`hasPlay` false): the playtime tiles, Hours played, the When-you-play playtime parts, Most played, Binged, Top game share, Tried and dropped and Sessions are left out; the Overview and When-you-play chips say why. The hub's Analytics strip stays All.

**Hide achievement farms** (checkbox next to the period, on by default; `?farms=show` turns it off) removes farm games from every section: unlocks, sessions, completions, and the per-game lists. A farm is a Steam game with `FARM_MIN_UNLOCKS` (10)+ unlocks earning more than `FARM_PER_HOUR` (15) achievements per hour of lifetime playtime (`games/index.json` `playtimeForever`). Real games stay at or under ~6/h; farms (Slash It, Lines X, …) run 20–700/h. The rule is Steam only (RA sets are curated, and Xbox has no playtime but a fixed 1,000 gamerscore per game); the admin (General → Farm Games, `../data/hub/farm-games.json`, `{ farms: ["xbox:123"], notFarms: ["steam:456"] }`) marks more by hand on any platform and overrules detected ones. A game is a farm when detected or marked, unless it's in `notFarms`. The hub's Analytics strip and Playing (`assets/playing.js`) read the same file. The checkbox tooltip lists them. The all-time funnel uses profile counts and is not filtered.

**Hide idle** (on by default; `?idle=show` turns it off) removes the games marked in the admin (General → Idle Games, `../data/hub/idle-games.json`, `{ games: ["ra:45053"] }`) through the same `keep()`: games left running (idle / clicker games), whose playtime isn't play and can't be told apart from real play by any rule (an idle clicker's sessions and unlock rate look like an RPG weekend). RA and Steam only in the admin: Xbox has no playtime. The hub's Analytics strip hides them too.

Playtime only exists since logging started (2026-03-26). Playtime tiles show "since …" when the period starts earlier, and their change vs the previous period is hidden unless that period was fully tracked.

## Layout: four tabs
The page is split into tabs (the profiles' `TabBar` + `useSwipeTabs` + `scrollToTabs` from `assets/tabs.js`: sticky, swipe on phones), kept in the URL as `?tab=` (absent = overview). Period, platform and farms stay in the header and scope every tab; the dates sit beside the title (`.an-range`, the timezone in its tooltip). The tab content is re-keyed on tab, period, platform, farm and idle changes so it fades. Each tab is one or two phone screens; the old single page was ~6,200px at 450px wide. Styles are the `.an-*` classes in `index.html`.

- **Overview:** the **Recap** card: two big numbers (Hours played, Unlocks, with their change) that are also the switch for the trend chart under them (`metric`; Xbox alone shows Unlocks only, with the "Xbox reports no playtime" chip), then a row of `Facts` (active days, sessions, avg session, and RA points / gamerscore / completed only when this or the previous period has any), and the key to the ▲ ▼ (`vs previous 30 days`) at the bottom, shown only when a change is. Then **highlights** (`.an-hl` buttons, each opens the tab with the detail): Top game (wide; the game's art behind it: Steam `library_hero` → `header`, RA the admin hero → banner from `data/ra/art.json`, Xbox `heroUrl`; else the shared library mosaic, `.art-icon` in `assets/ui.css`; `ArtBg`), with its time, share of play and a share bar; Peak time with the 24 hours as tiny bars (`MiniBars`, the peak lit); Favorite day with the 7 days; New games with up to 5 icons; Rarest unlock in its tier colour. Highlights with nothing to say are left out
- **Time:** **Daily rhythm** (`PlayClock`: a 24-hour ring of play shaded like the unlock grid, the typical start in the middle with a gold tick on the ring; beside it Peak time, Favorite day, Weekends, After midnight, Busiest day as rows; then the Time of day bars; from 1024px the bars sit beside the clock, `.an-rhythm-wrap`), **By hour** (`HourLines`: weekdays and weekends as two lines on one scale, weekends filled, with their day counts in the legend), **Per weekday**, **Sessions** (count · median · longest as the data line, the length histogram), **Unlocks by hour** (punchcard; cells capped at 20px tall so it doesn't balloon on desktop). Xbox alone: only the punchcard, with the chip
- **Games:** a `Facts` card (top game share, games per week, new games, tried and dropped), **Top games** with a switch (`Seg`): Most played · Most played · Most days (`Days played or unlocked`) · Progress (`Sets of 10+` `★ Completed`) · Binged (`Per day played` `2+ days · 2h+`), and **New games** with a switch New · Comebacks (`Back after 60+ days`) · Dropped (tried and dropped; counts in the switch; Xbox: New · Comebacks). List rows (`BarList`, `.an-brow`): 30px icon, title + RA marks, a sub line with a platform dot and the RA console or platform name, value, bar
- **Achievements:** **Rarity** (tier bars, rarest unlocks with the game icon), **Completion** (completed in the period with a star medal, the median days from first unlock and their icons; with none, the last completed game, how long ago, `agoLabel`; then the all-time funnels for the platforms in view, one card), **RetroAchievements by console** with a switch Hours played · Achievements
- **Loading:** `Skeleton({ tab })` draws the opened tab's own cards at their measured phone / desktop heights; when a tab's layout changes, update its skeleton too

### Metric rules
- **Recap:** hours, active days = days with play or an unlock, sessions, avg session, unlocks, RA points, gamerscore, completed, with % change; trend columns per day / week / month / year
- **When you play:** from the RA + Steam playtime grid (sessions split across local hours): peak time = busiest 2-hour window of the week, wrapping past midnight; favorite day; weekend share; after-midnight share 00–05; typical start = median session start on a clock starting at 05:00 so after-midnight play counts as late; busiest single day. **Time of day** (100% bars for all / RA / Steam, `DAY_PARTS` in utils: morning 05–12, afternoon 12–17, evening 17–22, night 22–05, one blue ramp light → dark). **Per weekday** (minutes on each weekday ÷ how many of that weekday the tracked part of the period has, stacked by platform). **By hour** (`HOUR_SERIES`): average minutes in each hour of a weekday / weekend day (minutes ÷ tracked days of that kind). **Unlocks by hour** (`Punchcard`): average unlocks per weekday-hour = unlocks on weekday d in hour h ÷ how many weekday-d days the range has; shade = √(value ÷ highest) cut at 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 so one burst hour doesn't dim everything (linear made wide ranges uniformly dim; rank/quartile coloring made rare hours look common), tooltip shows the average and the total. It's the only time-of-day view older than playtime tracking. Dropped: a playtime-by-hour punchcard, the Top unlock hours dial (it repeated the punchcard), separate weekday / weekend cards
- **Top games:** most played; **most days** (local days with a session or an unlock in each game, more hours first on ties: a habit vs a binge, which Most played can't show; Xbox counts its unlock days); **most progress** (share of each game's set earned in the period, sets of 10+, ties → bigger set, gold ★ if completed in the period); **binged** (minutes per day played, days with a session only; `BINGE_MIN_DAYS` (2)+ days and `BINGE_MIN_MINUTES` (120)+ in the period; RA/Steam). Dropped: **almost complete** (current progress, not the period's: the profiles' Progress → Nearly there covers it), **hardest** (playtime per unlock ranked games played for fun as hardest, an idle clicker left running on top; median rarity measured how far you got vs other players, mostly drop-off and obscurity, not difficulty) and "most unlocks" (it rewarded achievement farms)
- **Comebacks** (New games switch): the first session or unlock in the period after `COMEBACK_GAP_DAYS` (60)+ days with neither in that game (all time, `base.acts`), with `COMEBACK_MIN_MINUTES` (30)+ played or `COMEBACK_MIN_UNLOCKS` (3)+ unlocks since; most minutes since first, sub line "back after N months · N unlocks" (Xbox rows show unlocks as the value, `BarList` row `text`). Before playtime tracking only unlocks are known, so a gap starting there can hide sessions without unlocks. Periods are calendar based ("3 months" starts on the 1st), so a return late in the month before isn't in it
- **Focus:** top game share (of the period's playtime), games per week (distinct games played or unlocked in, averaged over the period's weeks), new games (first played or unlocked, all time, in the period; lifetime first/last activity and minutes come from `base.life`) with how many were completed, and **tried and dropped** (new in the period after tracking began, under `DROPPED_MAX_MINUTES` (60) played in total, untouched for `DROPPED_IDLE_DAYS` (30); RA/Steam only). Lists: new games by time played so far (★ completed in the period), tried and dropped by last played. A game played before tracking without an unlock can show as new at its first tracked session
- **Sessions:** length histogram (<30m … 4h+), longest session, median. Sessions of 5 minutes or less aren't logged by the pipelines
- **Rarity:** 100% stacked bar of rarity tiers per platform (site rarity colors, `RARITY_TIERS`), rarest unlocks in the period
- **Completion:** all-time funnel per platform (RA started → beaten → mastered; Steam owned → played → started → beaten → perfect; Xbox owned → started → beaten → completed). Steam/Xbox beaten = the win condition in `../data/{steam,xbox}/win-conditions.json` (same rule as the Completions page: `or` any / `and` all of the listed achievements) is met in the unlock history, plus every 100% game so Beaten never falls below Perfect/Completed, plus completions in the period and median days from a game's first unlock to completion
- **RA by console:** hours and achievements per console

## Charts (hand-written, no chart library)
- `StackedColumns` / `Column`: bars cap at 24px, 2px surface gap between segments, rounded top only, 3 recessive gridlines, labels thinned to ~8
- `TitleMarks`: RA subset badge + subset name + tilde tags (`TILDE_TAG_COLORS`), shown wherever an RA game name appears (Top games rows via `gameRow`'s `marks`, rarest unlocks, longest session); pair it with `parseTitle(name).baseTitle`
- `BarList`, `Punchcard` (`HEAT_RAMP`, one hue, √ scale), `PlayClock`, `HourLines`, `MiniBars`, `DayPartBar`, `RarityBar`, `Funnel` (one platform: label row, stage rows with bar, count and % kept from the stage before), `Facts` (numbers in a row, no tiles), `Seg` (switch inside a card), `Delta`, `ArtBg`
- Platform colors in fixed order (`PLATFORMS`: RA gold, Steam blue, Xbox green), so filtering never repaints a series. Text never uses series colors (except rarity % in the rarest list, matching the Steam page)
- One page-level fixed `Tooltip` via `TipContext`; marks get hover + keyboard focus through `useTip()`. Chart cards (`ChartCard`) have a **Table** toggle (a small icon button in the card's corner, `.an-tbtn`) so no value is hover-only
- Helpers live in `analytics/utils.js` (copies of Activity's `fmtMinutes`, `parseTitle`, `xboxImg`; pages don't share modules)

## Explanations
No grey sentence under titles. What a card counts and its rules are criteria chips (`Crits`, `.crit` in `assets/ui.css`): `ChartCard` takes `rules: [{ icon, text, gold }]` (falsy entries skipped), used sparingly (root CLAUDE.md, Info text): Rarity `% of players who have it`; in Top games the chips follow the switch (Progress `Sets of 10+ · ★ Completed`, Most days `Days played or unlocked`, Binged `Per day played · 2+ days · 2h+`; Comebacks `Back after 60+ days`). `ChartCard` also takes `pill` (a scope beside the title: "since …", "All time" on the funnels), `subtitle` only for data (the sessions line) and `right` (a control). The key to the change arrows (stacked green ▲ / red ▼, `vs previous 30 days`) sits at the bottom of the Recap. Don't repeat the period, platform or farm filter: the chips above already say it

**Hide menu** (`HideMenu`, `.fmenu*` in `../assets/ui.css`): farms and idle share one `Hide` chip in the platform row (count = games hidden now), which opens a checklist (Achievement farms, Idle games; each with its count, its first `HIDE_NAMES` (3) game names and "+N more", ordered by `hideNames`: playtime in the period, then unlocks). The menu is `position: fixed` under the chip because `.frow` scrolls sideways and would clip it; it closes on an outside tap, Escape, scroll or resize, and stays open while ticking
